# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project for
everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

Be constructive, welcome questions, respect privacy and attribution, and focus
feedback on the work rather than the person. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the channel described in
[SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove content or restrict
participation to protect the community.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and may be expanded before
the first stable release.
